About us

Parallax is a growing digital marketing firm serving small to medium-sized businesses. Since 2025, we’ve helped clients grow their brands and reach new customers using AI-powered strategy, content, and campaign tools. Our team of 8 people values reliability, respect, and open communication.

We believe a well-run team is the foundation of great work, and we’re looking for an organized, personable administrative assistant to help us keep it that way.

About the role

As our Administrative Assistant, you’ll be the heart of our daily operations. You’ll be the first point of contact for clients and callers, and a trusted resource for colleagues who need things to run smoothly. This is a varied, hands-on role where no two days are exactly alike.

You’ll work closely with our Founder, Project Manager, and marketing team, and you’ll have real opportunities to improve how we work, including finding smart ways to use AI tools to save everyone time. You’ll start with core administrative duties, and as you grow, you’ll have the chance to take on larger projects and more responsibility.

What you’ll do

Your day will start with our virtual front desk. From there, you’ll keep the team organized and the business running smoothly:

  • Virtual front desk: welcome clients to meetings, answer and direct calls and emails, and manage shared inboxes.
  • Team organization: manage calendars, schedule meetings, book travel, and take minutes when needed.
  • Documents and records: prepare, proofread, and format documents, keep our digital files in order, and protect the confidentiality of client and company records.
  • Vendors and finance: coordinate with vendors and software providers, process invoices and expense reports, and handle accurate data entry and basic bookkeeping tasks.
  • People and culture: help onboard new employees, plan team events, and back up colleagues during busy periods.

Where you’ll work

You’ll work remotely. Standard hours are Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. EST, with some flexibility.

Occasional overtime may be needed during busy periods, and it is paid in line with Alberta employment standards.

What you bring

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Experience with Microsoft Office and Google Workspace
  • Organization, attention to detail, and time management
  • Ability to prioritize competing tasks and stay calm under pressure
  • A professional, friendly manner with clients and colleagues alike
  • Discretion when handling confidential information
  • A proactive attitude, and comfort asking questions and suggesting improvements

Nice to have

  • Experience with AI tools (such as ChatGPT or Claude) or a strong interest in learning them
  • Bilingual in French and English
  • Experience in marketing or administration for small businesses
  • Familiarity with basic bookkeeping or invoicing
  • Event planning experience

What we offer

  • Salary: $20 to $30 per hour, depending on experience
  • Time off: 2 weeks of vacation, 15 personal or sick days, all statutory holidays, and an office closure between Christmas and New Year’s
  • Health: Group health, dental, and vision insurance starting after 3 months, with 50% of premiums paid by the company
  • Retirement: Pension with 50% employer matching
  • Growth: An annual training budget of $3,000 and clear opportunities to advance
  • Culture: A friendly, supportive team that values work-life balance
